# RPC Studio Instructor Term Sheet 2026: pay sections

Copied from the RPC Studio Instructor Term Sheet 2026 (RPC Pilates Studio, Las Piñas City; Google Doc last edited 29 Sep 2026), sections 3, 4 and 7, the parts that govern instructor pay. The instructor's personal details and the teaching sections are not copied. Where this sheet and Decision 75 (l) differ, Decision 75 (l) records what the product does and says so.

## 3. Two kinds of class (Clause 4.2 to 4.8)

Members never see the difference. On the booking page every class looks the same: a format, a time, six spots. Nobody is told that a class might not run. Core and flex is only about how the class is confirmed with you and how it is paid. Either way the rule is the same: if someone books it, it runs and you are paid for it.

**Core classes**

- Your fixed weekly classes, on the public timetable.
- The moment one person books, the class is locked in. It runs, and you are paid in full, even if that person cancels late or doesn't turn up. Their no-show is our problem, not yours.
- If nobody at all has booked 12 hours before the start, it doesn't run. We tell you straight away, and you are paid ₱400 for having held the hour.

**Flex classes**

- Sit immediately before or after a core class you are already teaching. They run only if someone books.
- We tell you by 8:00 PM the night before whether tomorrow's flex classes are on.
- If one isn't on, there's no pay and nothing to do: you were coming in for your core class anyway, so it never costs you a trip.
- If we ever want to give you a flex class that isn't next to one of your core classes, we have to agree a standby fee with you in writing first. Until we do, you're not obliged to take it.

A class with only one person in it runs as a semi-private, at no reduction in your fee. We will never ask you to teach for less because the room was quiet. We will not cancel a class just because only 1 person showed up.

## 4. What you are paid (Clause 6, Schedule A)

Your fee depends on how many people are booked 12 hours before the class starts. What happens after that (late cancellations, no-shows) never reduces it.

| Booked at the cutoff | You get |
|---|---|
| 0, class does not run | ₱400 core · ₱0 flex |
| 1 or 2 pax | ₱900 |
| 3 pax | ₱975 |
| 4 pax | ₱1,050 |
| 5 pax | ₱1,125 |
| 6 pax, full house | ₱1,400 |

Same rates for everyone, whatever your years of experience.

| Private sessions (intentionally booked by client/s) | You get |
|---|---|
| Private, one person | ₱1,000 |
| Duo | ₱1,400 |
| Trio | ₱1,600 |

- ₱500 bonus whenever someone whose first-ever class at RPC was taught by you goes on to buy a class pack or membership within 30 days. You don't need to sell anything or keep track: we work it out and it appears on your statement.
- Paid every two weeks, on the Friday after the period closes, by GCash or bank transfer.
- You get a statement listing every class, how many people were booked, and what you're owed. If anything looks wrong, tell us within 7 days and we'll sort it.

## 7. When a class can't go ahead (Clause 9)

| What happened | What you are paid |
|---|---|
| Nobody booked a core class | ₱400 |
| A flex class didn't confirm | Nothing: you weren't making an extra trip |
| We cancelled it: brownout, broken machine, our booking error | Your full base rate |
| Typhoon or government-declared suspension | Nothing: it affects us both equally |
| You didn't turn up, without notice or good reason | Nothing |

## Rates review (Clause 6.6)

Rates are reviewed first on 9 November 2027, then yearly. Any change to a rate applies going forward only, and only once the instructor has been told in writing. It never affects work already done.
